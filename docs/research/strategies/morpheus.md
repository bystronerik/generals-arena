# Morpheus strategy pointer

The Morpheus strategy specification is split into small topic files under
[`docs/bots/morpheus/`](../../bots/morpheus/index.md).

Morpheus is a competition-mode research bot. It owns the research axis
**learned belief-conditioned simultaneous-game planning**.

The diversity verdict is **distinct**. Morpheus does not claim or replace a
competition-roster axis. See the full
[diversity decision](../../bots/morpheus/diversity.md).

This pointer exists so the strategist and `build-bot-from-spec` workflows can
find the specification. The topic files remain the source of truth.
